Privacy & security

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 19, 2026

About 1Tap

1Tap is a personal expense tracker for Android, provided by Zerofrik. It helps you organize transaction information from bank messages on your device.

Information we access

With your permission, 1Tap reads relevant bank transaction SMS messages to identify amounts, merchants, dates, transaction types, and related details. This access is used only to provide the app's transaction-tracking functionality.

1Tap may also store the transaction records, categories, category keywords, and corrections that you create or approve in the app.

On-device processing

1Tap is designed to process and store your transaction data locally on your Android device. Your SMS content and transaction records are not uploaded to Zerofrik servers, sold, or shared with advertisers.

1Tap does not require a bank login or access to your bank account.

Purchases

Optional Pro purchases are processed by Google Play. Zerofrik does not receive or store your payment card, UPI, or bank-account details. Google Play may process purchase and entitlement information according to its own policies.

Your choices and deletion

You can deny or revoke SMS access in Android settings. You can also remove stored transaction data using the data-management controls in 1Tap. Revoking permission may prevent automatic transaction detection, but it does not delete data already stored locally.

Children's privacy

1Tap is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's functionality or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
