Why does 1Tap need transaction-message access?

Banks send SMS alerts when money moves in or out of an account. 1Tap uses supported transaction messages to identify eligible bank-account debits and credits automatically, so you don't have to enter every transaction manually.

check_circle
Used for
Identifying supported bank-account debit and credit transactions

Not for
Advertising, unrelated message profiling, or selling financial profiles
🇮🇳 Built for Indian Banking

Built around how money moves through Indian bank accounts

1Tap focuses on supported SMS messages that confirm money entering or leaving your bank account.

auto_awesome

Automatically detected when supported

  • Bank-account debits
  • Bank-account credits
  • UPI-linked bank debits
  • ATM cash withdrawals
  • Bank transfers (NEFT / IMPS / RTGS)
  • Salary and income bank credits
edit_note

Add manually when needed

  • Cash spending
  • Transactions without a supported bank SMS
  • Other personal income or expenses

Credit-card purchases and merchant notifications that do not prove bank-account cashflow are intentionally not treated as separate automatic expenses to prevent duplicate counting.
