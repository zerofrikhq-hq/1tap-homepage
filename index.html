Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Does 1Tap need my bank login?

No. 1Tap does not ask for your bank username, password or internet-banking credentials.

How does automatic tracking work?

1Tap checks supported bank transaction SMS on your Android phone and extracts eligible transaction details locally.

Is my financial data uploaded?

1Tap stores your transaction history locally on your device and does not require a cloud account.

Why does 1Tap need transaction-message access?

It uses supported bank transaction SMS to identify eligible bank-account debits and credits locally on your phone. Manual entry remains available.

Can I edit a transaction?

Yes. You can edit transaction details and categories. 1Tap can remember eligible corrections for future matching transactions.

Does 1Tap identify every merchant?

Merchant identification depends on the information available in the transaction message. When a merchant is unavailable or uncertain, you can correct the transaction manually.

Will transfers be counted as spending?

Money Out currently includes confirmed outgoing transactions, including transfers. Transfer-specific classification may be added in a future version.

Does 1Tap work offline?

Core expense tracking, categorization, analytics and local history do not depend on a cloud account or server connection.
